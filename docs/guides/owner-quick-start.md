# Owner quick start

**For:** a laundromat owner starting with DobiMaster.
**Time:** about 30 minutes, plus printing.
**Hardware:** none. Everything here works with QR stickers alone.

Nothing about your shop changes:
- **Coins and machines work exactly as before.**
- Customers don't install anything.
- If DobiMaster is ever down, the shop keeps running.

## Before you start

Have these ready:
- **Each machine:** washer or dryer, its size (kg), and your price for each programme.
- **Opening hours**, and the shop's WhatsApp number if you want customers to reach you.
- **A printer**, ideally with vinyl or laminated sticker paper (it lasts longer near water).

## 1. Create your account and set up the shop

Open `https://<your DobiMaster address>/owner` → **Create an account**. A four-step wizard follows:

1. **Your shop.**
   - Add the name, address and hours.
   - Tap **Use my location** while standing inside the shop, so customers find you on "nearby".
2. **Machines.**
   - Tap **+** for each size you have, e.g. 4 × Washer 10 kg, 2 × Dryer 15 kg.
   - Change the prices to match your price board.
   - The wizard shows the codes it will use (W1–W4, D1–D2). Number the machines in the shop the same way.
3. **QR stickers.**
   - Print the sticker sheet and put each sticker at eye level on its machine (W1 on W1…).
   - Also print the **customer poster** for the entrance. It explains how it works in BM, English and 中文.
4. **Go live.** Your shop now appears to customers. Until this step, it's hidden.

You can change anything later in **Shop settings** and on each machine's page.

## 2. Bring in your staff

1. **Staff → Add person.** Pick their role:
   - **Staff:** check-ins, tickets, checklists, cash. No revenue figures.
   - **Manager:** everything except managing staff.
2. **Staff → Staff guide.** Print it in BM, English or 中文 and put it in the back room.

## 3. Get told when something needs you

- On your phone, open the dashboard and tap **Get alerts on this phone**. You'll get alerts for faults, problem reports, sensors going offline and similar.
- **Weekly summary:** every Monday morning you get last week's cycles, revenue, problems and a "needs you" list by email.
  - Open **Weekly summary** to read past weeks, or to get it on WhatsApp too.

## 4. Your routine

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Every day | **Overview → Needs attention.** Deal with what's there. "All clear" means nothing needs you. |
| Every opening | Staff do the **Daily opening check** (Checklists). You can see who did it and when. |
| When a customer reports a problem | **Tickets.** Fix the machine, then resolve the ticket. For money claims, approve or reject the refund (**Refunds**) and pay it, e.g. by DuitNow, to the phone number the customer gave. |
| When you empty the coin boxes | **Collections → Record collection.** DobiMaster compares it with recorded use and flags shortfalls. |
| When maintenance is due | **Maintenance** lists what's due. Cleaning dryer lint ducts is also a fire-safety item. |

## 5. Tell your customers

- Share your **shop page link** on your WhatsApp status, Facebook page and your **Google Maps listing** (website field). It's at the top of **Shop settings**, with a Copy link button.
- The poster and stickers do the rest: customers scan, start a timer and get told when their laundry is almost done.

## 6. Later: live status with sensors (optional)

A small power sensor per machine turns "from check-ins" status into live, measured status. It also unlocks:
- "notify me when free",
- electricity cost per cycle,
- early warnings such as "heater may be failing".

Start with [sensors-buy-and-home-test.md](sensors-buy-and-home-test.md).

## Common questions

**Do customers need to download an app or sign up?**
No. They scan a QR code and it opens in their phone's browser. Their language (BM, English, 中文) is picked automatically and can be changed.

**Does it change or control my machines?**
No. Stickers and (optional) sensors only *show* what's happening. Paying in the app is a separate, later feature, only for machines that support it.

**Who sees customers' phone numbers and photos?**
Only you and your staff, for that report. They are deleted automatically:
- phone numbers 90 days after the case closes,
- photos 180 days after the case closes.

Customers can also delete their own data at any time. See the privacy notice at `/privacy`.

**What if the internet or DobiMaster is down?**
The shop works as before: coins and machines don't depend on it. Customers just won't get timers or alerts until it's back.
